THE LITTLE QUESTIONS

Good to know before you make.

How does the photo become a model?+

Upload a clear group photo, review everyone detected, then generate the whole scene. Poses, broad clothing shapes and recognized accessories come from local photo analysis. Unresolved people block incomplete output.

Is this an exact 3D scan of a person?+

No. Photo mode estimates visible body pose and creates an original stylized figure with simplified anatomy. Depth and hidden limbs are uncertain, and the app does not promise facial likeness.

Can I run it on my own Windows computer?+

Yes. Use the included Windows launcher after installing Python 3.12. It prepares the app and opens your browser. The first setup needs an internet connection to download dependencies and the local vision models. No hosting subscription is required.

Can I send the file straight to a 3D printer?+

Download a binary STL and open it in your slicer as millimeters: STL does not store units. Inspect the model, add supports if needed, and choose settings for your printer and material. Mesh checks are helpful heuristics, not a print-success guarantee.

Are these official branded figurines?+

No. These are original procedural designs with a rounded, minimalist aesthetic. Little Figures Studio uses no official character mesh, sculpt, logo, or packaging and claims no brand affiliation.
